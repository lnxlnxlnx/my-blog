# 电磁场与电磁波试卷完整解答
## 1、求矢量场的散度和旋度
### (1) $\vec{A}=(3x^2 y+y^2)\vec{e}_x+(xy^2-xz^3)\vec{e}_y+2xy\vec{e}_z$
#### 散度 $\nabla\cdot\vec{A}$
$$
\begin{align*}
\nabla\cdot\vec{A}&=\frac{\partial A_x}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial A_y}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial A_z}{\partial z}\\
&=\frac{\partial(3x^2y+y^2)}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial(xy^2-xz^3)}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial(2xy)}{\partial z}\\
&=6xy+2xy+0=\boldsymbol{8xy}
\end{align*}
$$

#### 旋度 $\nabla\times\vec{A}$
$$
\nabla\times\vec{A}=
\begin{vmatrix}
\vec{e}_x & \vec{e}_y & \vec{e}_z \\
\frac{\partial}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \\
3x^2y+y^2 & xy^2-xz^3 & 2xy
\end{vmatrix}
$$
$$
\begin{align*}
\vec{e}_x&:\frac{\partial(2xy)}{\partial y}-\frac{\partial(xy^2-xz^3)}{\partial z}=2x+3xz^2\\
\vec{e}_y&:-\left[\frac{\partial(2xy)}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial(3x^2y+y^2)}{\partial z}\right]=-(2y-0)=-2y\\
\vec{e}_z&:\frac{\partial(xy^2-xz^3)}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial(3x^2y+y^2)}{\partial y}=(y^2-z^3)-(3x^2+2y)=y^2-z^3-3x^2-2y
\end{align*}
$$
$$
\nabla\times\vec{A}=\boldsymbol{(2x+3xz^2)\vec{e}_x-2y\vec{e}_y+(y^2-z^3-3x^2-2y)\vec{e}_z}
$$

### (2) $\vec{A}=yz\vec{e}_x+xz\vec{e}_y+xy^2\vec{e}_z$
#### 散度 $\nabla\cdot\vec{A}$
$$
\nabla\cdot\vec{A}=\frac{\partial(yz)}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial(xz)}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial(xy^2)}{\partial z}=0+0+0=\boldsymbol{0}
$$

#### 旋度 $\nabla\times\vec{A}$
$$
\nabla\times\vec{A}=
\begin{vmatrix}
\vec{e}_x & \vec{e}_y & \vec{e}_z \\
\frac{\partial}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \\
yz & xz & xy^2
\end{vmatrix}
$$
$$
\begin{align*}
\vec{e}_x&:\frac{\partial(xy^2)}{\partial y}-\frac{\partial(xz)}{\partial z}=2xy-x\\
\vec{e}_y&:-\left[\frac{\partial(xy^2)}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial(yz)}{\partial z}\right]=-(y^2-y)=y-y^2\\
\vec{e}_z&:\frac{\partial(xz)}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial(yz)}{\partial y}=z-z=0
\end{align*}
$$
$$
\nabla\times\vec{A}=\boldsymbol{(2xy-x)\vec{e}_x+(y-y^2)\vec{e}_y}
$$

## 2、证明：(1) $\nabla\times(\nabla\phi)=0$；(2) $\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\vec{B})=0$
### (1) 标量梯度的旋度恒为0
设 $\nabla\phi=\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial x}\vec{e}_x+\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial y}\vec{e}_y+\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial z}\vec{e}_z$
$$
\nabla\times(\nabla\phi)=
\begin{vmatrix}
\vec{e}_x & \vec{e}_y & \vec{e}_z \\
\frac{\partial}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \\
\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial\phi}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial\phi}{\partial z}
\end{vmatrix}
$$
$\vec{e}_x$ 分量：$\displaystyle \frac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial y\partial z}-\frac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial z\partial y}=0$
$\vec{e}_y$ 分量：$\displaystyle -\left(\frac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial x\partial z}-\frac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial z\partial x}\right)=0$
$\vec{e}_z$ 分量：$\displaystyle \frac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial x\partial y}-\frac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial y\partial x}=0$
混合偏导顺序可交换，故 $\boldsymbol{\nabla\times(\nabla\phi)=0}$。

### (2) 矢量旋度的散度恒为0
设 $\vec{B}=B_x\vec{e}_x+B_y\vec{e}_y+B_z\vec{e}_z$，先算旋度：
$$
\nabla\times\vec{B}=
\left(\frac{\partial B_z}{\partial y}-\frac{\partial B_y}{\partial z}\right)\vec{e}_x
-\left(\frac{\partial B_z}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial B_x}{\partial z}\right)\vec{e}_y
+\left(\frac{\partial B_y}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial B_x}{\partial y}\right)\vec{e}_z
$$
再求散度：
$$
\begin{align*}
\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\vec{B})
&=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial B_z}{\partial y}-\frac{\partial B_y}{\partial z}\right)
-\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial B_z}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial B_x}{\partial z}\right)
+\frac{\partial}{\partial z}\left(\frac{\partial B_y}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial B_x}{\partial y}\right)\\
&=\frac{\partial^2 B_z}{\partial x\partial y}-\frac{\partial^2 B_y}{\partial x\partial z}
-\frac{\partial^2 B_z}{\partial y\partial x}+\frac{\partial^2 B_x}{\partial y\partial z}
+\frac{\partial^2 B_y}{\partial z\partial x}-\frac{\partial^2 B_x}{\partial z\partial y}\\
&=0
\end{align*}
$$
混合偏导抵消，故 $\boldsymbol{\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\vec{B})=0}$。

## 3、无耗均匀传输线，$Z_0=50\,\Omega,\varepsilon_r=2.25,\mu_r=1$，$f=100\,\text{MHz}$，线长 $l=\lambda/4$，负载 $R_L=1\,\Omega$
### 基础参数计算
$$
c=3\times10^8\,\text{m/s},\quad v_p=\frac{c}{\sqrt{\varepsilon_r}}=\frac{3\times10^8}{1.5}=2\times10^8\,\text{m/s}
$$
$$
\lambda=\frac{v_p}{f}=\frac{2\times10^8}{100\times10^6}=2\,\text{m},\quad l=\frac{\lambda}{4}=0.5\,\text{m}
$$
相位常数：$\displaystyle \beta=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}=\pi\ \text{rad/m}$

### (1) 传输线实际长度
$$
\boldsymbol{l=\frac{\lambda}{4}=0.5\,\text{m}}
$$

### (2) 负载终端反射系数 $\Gamma_L$
$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{Z_L-Z_0}{Z_L+Z_0}=\frac{1-50}{1+50}=\boldsymbol{-\frac{49}{51}\approx-0.9608}
$$

### (3) 输入反射系数 $\Gamma_{in}$
无耗线输入反射系数：$\Gamma_{in}=\Gamma_L e^{-j2\beta l}$
$$
2\beta l=2\cdot\pi\cdot\frac{\lambda}{4}=\frac{\pi\lambda}{2}\bigg|_{\lambda=2}=\pi,\quad e^{-j\pi}=-1
$$
$$
\Gamma_{in}=\Gamma_L\cdot(-1)=\boldsymbol{\frac{49}{51}\approx0.9608}
$$

### (4) 输入端阻抗 $Z_{in}$
$\lambda/4$ 阻抗变换公式：$\displaystyle Z_{in}=\frac{Z_0^2}{Z_L}$
$$
Z_{in}=\frac{50^2}{1}=\boldsymbol{2500\,\Omega}
$$
验证：$Z_{in}=Z_0\frac{1+\Gamma_{in}}{1-\Gamma_{in}}=50\cdot\frac{1+49/51}{1-49/51}=50\cdot\frac{100/51}{2/51}=2500\,\Omega$，一致。

## 4、无耗线 $Z_0=50\,\Omega$，线上电压最大值 $U_{\text{max}}=100\,\text{mV}$，最小值 $U_{\text{min}}=20\,\text{mV}$；第一个电压波节点距负载 $d_{\text{min1}}=\lambda/3$，求负载阻抗 $Z_L$
### 步骤1：驻波比 $\rho$
$$
\rho=\frac{U_{\text{max}}}{U_{\text{min}}}=\frac{100}{20}=5
$$

### 步骤2：反射系数模 $|\Gamma_L|$
$$
|\Gamma_L|=\frac{\rho-1}{\rho+1}=\frac{5-1}{5+1}=\frac{4}{6}=\frac{2}{3}
$$

### 步骤3：反射系数相位 $\varphi_L$
电压波节点位置公式：$\displaystyle d_{\text{min1}}=\frac{\varphi_L}{4\pi}\lambda+\frac{\lambda}{4}$
变形求相位：
$$
\frac{\lambda}{3}=\frac{\varphi_L \lambda}{4\pi}+\frac{\lambda}{4}
\Rightarrow \frac{1}{3}-\frac{1}{4}=\frac{\varphi_L}{4\pi}
\Rightarrow \frac{1}{12}=\frac{\varphi_L}{4\pi}
\Rightarrow \varphi_L=\frac{\pi}{3}
$$

### 步骤4：负载阻抗
$$
\Gamma_L=|\Gamma_L|e^{j\varphi_L}=\frac{2}{3}e^{j\pi/3}
$$
$$
Z_L=Z_0\cdot\frac{1+\Gamma_L}{1-\Gamma_L}
$$
代入复数计算：$e^{j\pi/3}=0.5+j\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
$$
1+\Gamma_L=1+\frac{2}{3}\left(\frac12+j\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)=\frac{4}{3}+j\frac{\sqrt3}{3}
$$
$$
1-\Gamma_L=1-\frac{2}{3}\left(\frac12+j\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)=\frac{2}{3}-j\frac{\sqrt3}{3}
$$
$$
Z_L=50\cdot\frac{4+j\sqrt3}{2-j\sqrt3}
=50\cdot\frac{(4+j\sqrt3)(2+j\sqrt3)}{2^2+(\sqrt3)^2}
=50\cdot\frac{8+4j\sqrt3+2j\sqrt3-3}{7}
$$
$$
=50\cdot\frac{5+j6\sqrt3}{7}
=\boldsymbol{\frac{250+j300\sqrt3}{7}\approx35.71+j74.23\,\Omega}
$$

## 5、已知复矢量 $\dot{\vec{E}}(z)=\vec{e}_x E_0\sin(k_0 z)\ \text{V/m}$，$\dot{\vec{H}}(z)=\vec{e}_y\sqrt{\frac{\varepsilon_0}{\mu_0}}E_0\cos(k_0 z)\ \text{A/m}$，$k_0=\omega\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}=\omega/c$
### (1) 时谐矢量 $\vec{E}(z,t),\vec{H}(z,t)$
时谐场公式：$\vec{F}(z,t)=\text{Re}\big[\dot{\vec{F}}(z)e^{j\omega t}\big]$
$$
\dot{\vec{E}}=\vec{e}_x E_0\cdot\frac{e^{jk_0 z}-e^{-jk_0 z}}{2j}
$$
$$
\vec{E}(z,t)=\text{Re}\left[\vec{e}_x E_0\sin(k_0 z)e^{j\omega t}\right]
=\boldsymbol{\vec{e}_x E_0\sin(k_0 z)\cos(\omega t)}
$$
$$
\vec{H}(z,t)=\text{Re}\left[\vec{e}_y\sqrt{\frac{\varepsilon_0}{\mu_0}}E_0\cos(k_0 z)e^{j\omega t}\right]
=\boldsymbol{\vec{e}_y \sqrt{\frac{\varepsilon_0}{\mu_0}}E_0\cos(k_0 z)\cos(\omega t)}
$$

### (2) $z=0,z=\lambda/8$ 瞬时坡印廷矢量 $\vec{S}(z,t)=\vec{E}\times\vec{H}$
#### ① $z=0$
$\sin0=0,\ \cos0=1$：$\vec{E}(0,t)=0,\quad \boldsymbol{\vec{S}(0,t)=0}$

#### ② $z=\lambda/8$
$k_0 z=\displaystyle \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}\cdot\frac{\lambda}{8}=\frac{\pi}{4}$
$$
\sin\frac{\pi}{4}=\cos\frac{\pi}{4}=\frac{\sqrt2}{2}
$$
$$
\vec{E}=\vec{e}_x E_0\cdot\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\cos\omega t,\quad
\vec{H}=\vec{e}_y\sqrt{\frac{\varepsilon_0}{\mu_0}}E_0\cdot\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\cos\omega t
$$
$$
\vec{S}=\vec{E}\times\vec{H}=
\vec{e}_x\times\vec{e}_y \cdot \frac{\sqrt2}{2}E_0\cos\omega t \cdot \frac{\sqrt2}{2}E_0\sqrt{\frac{\varepsilon_0}{\mu_0}}\cos\omega t
$$
$$
=\boldsymbol{\vec{e}_z \cdot \frac{1}{2}E_0^2\sqrt{\frac{\varepsilon_0}{\mu_0}}\cos^2(\omega t)}
$$

### (3) 时间平均坡印廷矢量 $\langle\vec{S}(z,t)\rangle$
平均坡印廷：$\displaystyle \langle\vec{S}\rangle=\frac12\text{Re}\big[\dot{\vec{E}}\times\dot{\vec{H}}^*\big]$
$$
\dot{\vec{E}}=E_0\sin k_0 z\ \vec{e}_x,\quad
\dot{\vec{H}}^*=\sqrt{\frac{\varepsilon_0}{\mu_0}}E_0\cos k_0 z\ \vec{e}_y
$$
$$
\dot{\vec{E}}\times\dot{\vec{H}}^*=E_0^2\sqrt{\frac{\varepsilon_0}{\mu_0}}\sin k_0 z \cos k_0 z\ \vec{e}_z
$$
$$
\langle\vec{S}\rangle=\frac12 E_0^2\sqrt{\frac{\varepsilon_0}{\mu_0}}\sin k_0 z \cos k_0 z\ \vec{e}_z
=\boldsymbol{\frac{E_0^2}{4}\sqrt{\frac{\varepsilon_0}{\mu_0}}\sin(2k_0 z)\ \vec{e}_z}
$$

## 6、自由空间均匀平面波电场：$\dot{\vec{E}}=\vec{e}_x 10^{-4}e^{-j20\pi z}+\vec{e}_y j10^{-4}e^{-j20\pi z}\ \text{V/m}$
### (1) 传播方向与频率
相位因子 $e^{-j\beta z}$，传播方向：$\boldsymbol{+\vec{e}_z}$
自由空间 $\beta=k_0=\omega/c=20\pi$
$$
k_0=\frac{2\pi f}{c}=20\pi \Rightarrow f=\frac{20\pi\cdot c}{2\pi}=10c
$$
$$
\boldsymbol{f=10\times3\times10^8=3\times10^9\ \text{Hz}=3\ \text{GHz}}
$$

### (2) 极化方式
$\dot{E}_x=10^{-4}e^{-j20\pi z},\ \dot{E}_y=j10^{-4}e^{-j20\pi z}=10^{-4}e^{j\pi/2}e^{-j20\pi z}$
$E_x,E_y$ 幅度相等，$y$ 分量相位超前 $x$ 分量 $\pi/2$；沿$+z$传播，为**左旋圆极化波**。

### (3) 磁场强度 $\dot{\vec{H}}$
均匀平面波关系：$\displaystyle \dot{\vec{H}}=\frac{1}{\eta_0}\vec{e}_k\times\dot{\vec{E}},\ \eta_0=\sqrt{\frac{\mu_0}{\varepsilon_0}}\approx120\pi\,\Omega,\ \vec{e}_k=\vec{e}_z$
$$
\vec{e}_z\times\vec{e}_x=\vec{e}_y,\quad \vec{e}_z\times\vec{e}_y=-\vec{e}_x
$$
$$
\dot{\vec{H}}=\frac{1}{\eta_0}\big[\vec{e}_z\times(\vec{e}_x 10^{-4}e^{-j20\pi z}+j\vec{e}_y 10^{-4}e^{-j20\pi z})\big]
$$
$$
=\frac{10^{-4}}{\eta_0}\big(\vec{e}_y-j\vec{e}_x\big)e^{-j20\pi z}
=\boldsymbol{\frac{10^{-4}}{120\pi}\left(-j\vec{e}_x+\vec{e}_y\right)e^{-j20\pi z}\ \text{A/m}}
$$

### (4) 时间平均坡印廷矢量 $\langle\vec{S}\rangle$
$$
\langle\vec{S}\rangle=\frac12\text{Re}\big[\dot{\vec{E}}\times\dot{\vec{H}}^*\big]
$$
$$
\dot{\vec{E}}=10^{-4}(\vec{e}_x+j\vec{e}_y)e^{-j20\pi z},\quad
\dot{\vec{H}}^*=\frac{10^{-4}}{\eta_0}(-j\vec{e}_x+\vec{e}_y)e^{j20\pi z}
$$
$$
\dot{\vec{E}}\times\dot{\vec{H}}^*=\frac{10^{-8}}{\eta_0}\left[
\vec{e}_x\times(-j\vec{e}_x)+\vec{e}_x\times\vec{e}_y
+j\vec{e}_y\times(-j\vec{e}_x)+j\vec{e}_y\times\vec{e}_y
\right]
$$
叉乘化简：$\vec{e}_x\times\vec{e}_y=\vec{e}_z,\ \vec{e}_y\times\vec{e}_x=-\vec{e}_z$
$$
=\frac{10^{-8}}{\eta_0}\big[\vec{e}_z + \vec{e}_z\big]=\frac{2\times10^{-8}}{\eta_0}\vec{e}_z
$$
$$
\langle\vec{S}\rangle=\frac12\cdot\frac{2\times10^{-8}}{120\pi}\vec{e}_z
=\boldsymbol{\frac{10^{-8}}{120\pi}\vec{e}_z\ \text{W/m}^2}
$$

## 7、自由空间均匀平面波磁场：$\vec{H}(z,t)=(\vec{e}_x+\vec{e}_y)0.8\cos(6\pi\times10^8 t-2\pi z)\ \text{A/m}$
### (1) 频率、波长、相位常数、相速
对比时谐形式 $\cos(\omega t-\beta z)$：
$$
\omega=6\pi\times10^8\ \text{rad/s},\quad \beta=2\pi\ \text{rad/m}
$$
- 频率：$\displaystyle f=\frac{\omega}{2\pi}=\frac{6\pi\times10^8}{2\pi}=\boldsymbol{3\times10^8\ \text{Hz}=300\ \text{MHz}}$
- 相位常数：$\boldsymbol{\beta=2\pi\ \text{rad/m}}$
- 波长：$\displaystyle \lambda=\frac{2\pi}{\beta}=\boldsymbol{1\ \text{m}}$
- 自由空间相速：$\boldsymbol{v_p=c=3\times10^8\ \text{m/s}}$

### (2) 电场强度 $\vec{E}(z,t)$
本征阻抗 $\eta_0=120\pi$，$\vec{e}_k=\vec{e}_z$，$\dot{\vec{H}}=0.8(\vec{e}_x+\vec{e}_y)e^{-j2\pi z}$
$$
\dot{\vec{E}}=\eta_0 \dot{\vec{H}}\times\vec{e}_k
$$
$$
\dot{\vec{E}}=120\pi\cdot 0.8(\vec{e}_x+\vec{e}_y)e^{-j2\pi z}\times\vec{e}_z
=96\pi\left(-\vec{e}_y+\vec{e}_x\right)e^{-j2\pi z}
$$
瞬时场：
$$
\boldsymbol{\vec{E}(z,t)=96\pi(\vec{e}_x-\vec{e}_y)\cos(6\pi\times10^8 t-2\pi z)\ \text{V/m}}
$$

### (3) 瞬时坡印廷矢量 $\vec{S}(z,t)=\vec{E}\times\vec{H}$
$$
\begin{align*}
\vec{E}&=96\pi(\vec{e}_x-\vec{e}_y)\cos\theta,\quad \theta=6\pi\times10^8 t-2\pi z\\
\vec{H}&=0.8(\vec{e}_x+\vec{e}_y)\cos\theta
\end{align*}
$$
$$
\begin{align*}
\vec{S}&=96\pi\cdot0.8\cdot\cos^2\theta \cdot
(\vec{e}_x-\vec{e}_y)\times(\vec{e}_x+\vec{e}_y)\\
&=76.8\pi \cos^2\theta \cdot (\vec{e}_x\times\vec{e}_y-\vec{e}_y\times\vec{e}_x)\\
&=76.8\pi \cos^2\theta \cdot (\vec{e}_z+\vec{e}_z)
\end{align*}
$$
$$
\boldsymbol{\vec{S}(z,t)=153.6\pi \cos^2(6\pi\times10^8 t-2\pi z)\ \vec{e}_z\ \text{W/m}^2}
$$

### (4) 时间平均坡印廷矢量 $\langle\vec{S}\rangle$
$\langle\cos^2\theta\rangle=\displaystyle \frac12$，故：
$$
\langle\vec{S}\rangle=153.6\pi\cdot\frac12 \vec{e}_z
=\boldsymbol{76.8\pi\ \vec{e}_z\ \text{W/m}^2}
$$
